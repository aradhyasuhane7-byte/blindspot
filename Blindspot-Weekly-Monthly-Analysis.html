What do these statuses mean?
OverdueThe order’s current step has gone past its time limit. The extra minutes count as lost time. Press Examine to record why.
At risk75% or more of the current step’s time is used, but it is still inside the limit. Act now to avoid a delay.
On trackThe current step has used less than 75% of its time limit, so nothing needs doing yet.
DeliveredAll four steps (Confirm, Pack, Dispatch, Delivery) are marked done. It can still show lost minutes if an earlier step ran over.
Progress bar: green is a finished step, blue is in progress and on track, amber is at risk, red is overdue, grey has not started yet. Minutes lost is the time beyond a step’s limit, counted against the step where it happened.

About Blindspot

About this website

Delay Finder follows every order through four steps: Confirm, Pack, Dispatch and Delivery. Each step has its own time limit. The Queue shows which orders are on track, at risk or overdue right now. The Weekly analysis shows how many minutes were lost, at which step and with which person.

A note from Aradhya Suhane

Most late deliveries are not one big failure. They are small waits at a step nobody was watching, and a seller usually finds out only when a buyer complains. I started Blindspot so that one screen shows a delay while there is still time to fix it.

Our intention

  • To solve the delay problem sellers face every day, one late order at a time.
  • To show sellers the one step to fix first, instead of blaming the courier by default.
  • To keep delays visible to the whole team, not hidden in a spreadsheet.
  • To stay simple enough to use on a busy packing floor.

What we believe

A delay you can see is a delay you can fix. Every late order is a clue, and the clues add up to a calmer, more reliable week for the seller and a happier buyer at the door.

Aradhya Suhane and the Blindspot team
